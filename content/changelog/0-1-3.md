The release that made a site's navigation content. `content/nav/header.yaml` and `footer.yaml` are the two menus, a `ref` resolves against the site's routes at build and a dead one is lint rule 5, and both shipped themes render them. Under it, the shell became a part a theme overrides by name, `theme.yaml` is validated when it loads, and the social metadata every page carries moved into core.

Also in this version: the agent publishes and the agent pushes — `site › push` under the site's `deploy.push` policy, which is where the human stays — and the bench of record comes from CI on this tree rather than from a laptop. The three launch posts were written through the MCP in this window, and the six things the writing path found each got a test.
